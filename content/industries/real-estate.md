## Where AI actually helps in real estate and property

Property management is administrative work that arrives in small pieces: a tenant asking about a heating issue, a landlord wanting a statement, an invoice to match, a lease to check. According to one industry summary, property managers spend between 25 and 40 percent of their week on administrative activities, with work order management, invoice processing and vendor coordination named as the main time drains.

The Swiss sector is digitalising, but unevenly. The Digital Real Estate 2026 study by pom+ rates the construction and real estate industry at 4.3 out of 10 for digital maturity, up from 4.0 in 2025, with property managers at their highest score yet (4.4). About two thirds of respondents see significant benefits from AI, down from 75 percent the year before. The study says investment and cost are the obstacles named most often, not culture or staffing.

That matches what we would expect: AI pays off where a task is frequent, text-based and rule-governed, and it disappoints where it is asked to replace judgement. Deloitte's 2026 commercial real estate outlook notes that generative AI can summarise standard leases well but may struggle with unusual lease terms, and that results improve with human review.

## What we build

### Tenant inquiry chatbot
A multilingual chatbot on your website or tenant portal that answers common questions (house rules, move-out steps, waste collection, who to call), and collects repair reports with photos and the right unit number. Anything unclear goes to a property manager with the full conversation.

### Repair and work order triage
An agent that reads incoming reports, classifies urgency (burst pipe versus squeaky door), suggests the right tradesperson from your vendor list and drafts the work order. A manager approves before a vendor is instructed.

### Lease and document extraction
An agent that reads leases, amendments and house-rule documents, extracts dates, notice periods, rent adjustments and indexation clauses into a structured register, and points to the source passage so a person can check each value quickly.

### Invoice matching
A workflow that reads supplier invoices, matches them to orders and properties, and flags odd amounts or duplicates for review before they enter accounting.

### Cloud setup for property data
We connect these agents to your property management software, mailboxes and document storage, with access rules that keep each owner's and tenant's data separate.

## Compliance and data considerations

Tenant data is personal data. The revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP/revDSG) has applied since 1 September 2023, and the FDPIC says it applies directly to AI-supported processing and calls for transparency on purpose and data sources. People may also ask for automated decisions to be reviewed by a human, so we do not let an agent make decisions about tenants on its own.

Lex Koller, the federal act on acquisition of real estate by persons abroad, matters if you handle transactions. According to the Federal Office of Justice, it applies to foreign nationals, companies domiciled abroad and Swiss companies under foreign control, and the competent cantonal authority decides whether authorisation is needed. Unauthorised acquisitions are null and void. The Federal Department of Justice and Police opened a consultation on tightening the rules on 15 April 2026, according to a summary of recent developments. We would not have an agent decide whether a transaction is covered; at most it would collect the buyer facts and flag the case for a lawyer or notary.

If you serve EU tenants or buyers, the GDPR applies, and the EU AI Act requires chatbots to disclose that they are AI. Our default is hosting in Switzerland or the EU, audit logs of agent actions and human approval for anything with legal or financial effect.

## How an engagement starts

We would start with one building portfolio and one workflow, usually tenant inquiries or document extraction. We run a small pilot, measure how many requests are resolved without staff and how accurate the extracted data is, then decide together whether to extend it. If the pilot does not show a clear gain, we will tell you.
