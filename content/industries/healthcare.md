## Where AI actually helps in healthcare and life sciences

Clinical work generates a lot of writing. In a recent survey of Swiss doctors run by the JRC Bern research institute for the FMH, daily documentation time in acute care had risen from 114 to almost 120 minutes, while direct patient contact averaged about 102 minutes a day. Doctors named record writing, reports and insurance correspondence among their most time-consuming administrative tasks.

The picture abroad is similar. A survey of 966 NHS staff by Nuance and Ignetica found clinicians spending an average of 13.5 hours a week on clinical documentation, more than a third of their working hours. Both are surveys with their own limits, but they point the same way: administration competes with care.

Life sciences companies face a parallel burden in quality documents, regulatory submissions and deviation handling. Swissmedic notes that AI models can help across drug development, manufacturing and regulatory processes, while their complexity and lack of transparency make data quality, bias and quality control hard to assess. That argues for narrow, supervised use rather than broad autonomy.

## What we build

### Administrative front-desk chatbot
A chatbot for appointment requests, opening hours, preparation instructions and routine questions, in several languages. It does not give medical advice, and it hands over to staff for anything clinical or urgent.

### Documentation drafting assistant
An agent that turns approved inputs, such as a dictated note or structured fields, into a draft report or letter in your template. The clinician reviews, edits and signs. Whether a specific drafting tool needs medical-device assessment depends on its intended purpose, and we would clarify that with you before building.

### Insurer correspondence and billing preparation
An agent that gathers the supporting facts for insurer queries and prepares draft replies and coding suggestions for staff to check, reducing a task doctors say they find burdensome.

### Quality and regulatory document assistant
For labs, pharma and medtech firms, a private assistant that searches SOPs, quality manuals and past deviations, drafts summaries, and shows the source for every statement. Controlled documents stay under your document-control process.

### Secure cloud foundation
Hosting in Switzerland or the EU, with separation of patient-identifiable data, access controls and complete logging.

## Compliance and data considerations

Patient data is sensitive personal data. The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP/revDSG) applies directly to AI-supported processing, according to the FDPIC, including transparency about purpose and data sources, and the right to have automated individual decisions reviewed by a human. Health professionals also carry professional secrecy duties, so we would involve your data protection and legal contacts early.

Medical devices are a separate track. Swiss law applies EU MDR requirements through the Medical Devices Ordinance (MepV), and the Swiss Federal Council has said it will keep developing sector-specific AI rules, with healthcare named explicitly. Software with a medical purpose can be a regulated device, so we keep our scope on administrative and documentation support unless you want to pursue a device pathway with the right regulatory partners. One article on Swiss medical device cybersecurity reports that, under the Digital Omnibus amendment, AI Act high-risk requirements for medical devices apply from 2 August 2028.

For GxP environments, Swissmedic follows international guidance from bodies such as the EMA, ICH, WHO and FDA, and expects supervision across the whole life cycle. We would design with audit trails, version control of prompts and models, and human approval of anything entering a regulated record. The AI Act also lists systems that evaluate eligibility for healthcare services and medical triage as high-risk, which is one reason we avoid those uses.

## How an engagement starts

We start with a short scoping call and one narrow pilot, for example drafting a single type of letter, with time per document measured before and after. If the result is modest, we will tell you rather than stretch the project. If it works, we extend to the next workflow with your quality and data protection teams involved.
