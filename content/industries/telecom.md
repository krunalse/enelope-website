## Where AI actually helps in telecom and utilities

Telecom providers and utilities have a lot of customers, a lot of repetitive questions and strict rules about what they may do with customer data. Billing queries, plan changes, outage reports and move-in or move-out requests make up much of the volume, and most follow a small number of patterns.

Deloitte frames agentic AI as a large opportunity for telcos, projecting 150 billion US dollars over five years, and names network management, billing and customer service as the main areas. That is a vendor projection and should be read as such. What it does show is where the industry itself sees the work: customer care and operations, not futuristic features.

For Swiss utilities the data side is growing fast. Under the federal act on a secure electricity supply from renewable energy sources, the nationwide smart meter mandate started on 1 January 2025, with a second regulatory package in force since 1 January 2026. Smart meters record consumption at 15-minute intervals, which means more customer questions about bills and profiles, and more data that must be handled carefully.

## What we build

### Customer care chatbot
A chatbot that answers questions about invoices, tariffs, outages and meter readings by looking up the customer's own account after authentication. It handles the routine cases and passes the rest to a human agent with a summary, so customers do not have to repeat themselves.

### Agent assist for the contact centre
A tool that sits beside your human agents, reads the incoming email or call transcript, suggests an answer from your knowledge base and fills in the ticket. The agent edits and sends; nothing goes out unreviewed.

### Outage and fault ticket triage
An agent that groups incoming fault reports by area and symptom, matches them to known incidents and drafts status updates for affected customers. Network engineers keep the decisions; the agent reduces the sorting work.

### Contract and invoice document handling
A workflow that reads customer letters, change-of-address forms and supplier invoices, extracts the relevant fields and creates drafts in your billing or CRM system for staff to confirm.

### Cloud infrastructure
We build the hosting, integration and monitoring that connect these agents to your CRM, billing and ticketing systems, with role-based access and full logging.

## Compliance and data considerations

Customer usage data is sensitive. According to MME Legal, 15-minute smart meter load profiles are not unlawful profiling in themselves, but analysing them to evaluate personal aspects can change that. Smart metering systems must also pass security tests by METAS or approved third parties. Our agents work with the minimum data a task needs, and we would not build analysis of household behaviour without a clear legal basis and a data protection impact assessment.

The revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP/revDSG) applies directly to AI, according to the FDPIC. It asks for transparency about purpose and data sources, and lets people request human review of automated decisions. The GDPR applies where you serve EU customers. Telecom providers are also subject to the Swiss Telecommunications Act (FMG), supervised by ComCom and the Federal Office of Communications (OFCOM), and any deployment should be reviewed against your own regulatory obligations with your legal team.

Under the EU AI Act, chatbots must tell users they are talking to an AI (Article 50, applicable since 2 August 2026). We add this disclosure by default, host data in Switzerland or the EU, keep audit logs of every agent action and require human approval for contract changes, credits and disconnections.

## How an engagement starts

We start with one contact reason, such as invoice questions or outage status, and run a scoped pilot on real, anonymised tickets. We measure how many are resolved correctly without a person and how often the agent hands over, then decide whether to extend it. If the volume or data quality is not there, we will say so before you spend more.
