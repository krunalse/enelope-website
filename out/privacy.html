Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

01Overview

This policy explains what information Enelope ("we", "us") collects through enelope.ch, how we use it, and who we share it with. It covers this website only — separate agreements govern any client engagement or project work.

02Information we collect

We collect information in one way:

  • Contact form. The contact form opens an email in your own email app, addressed to us, with the name, email, company, phone number, service of interest, and message you provide. Nothing is sent to our servers.
  • Basic hosting logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

All other content on this site (service descriptions, case studies, testimonials) is static and does not involve collecting any information from visitors.

03How we use your information

We use contact form submissions solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for marketing unless you separately opt in.

04Third-party service providers

We rely on a small number of subprocessors to run this site:

  • Vercel — hosts this website (privacy policy).

05Cookies

We don't use tracking, advertising, or any other cookies on this site.

06Data retention

Contact form submissions are sent from your own email app directly to our team's inbox and never pass through or are stored on our servers. Services, case studies, and testimonials content shown on this site is static and maintained directly in the site's source code.

07Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or request that we delete it, by emailing hello@enelope.ch.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

09Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@enelope.ch.
